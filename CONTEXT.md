# Webtix showcase context

## Purpose

`navrhy.webtix.cz` is the curated showcase of Webtix website proposals, concepts and experiments.

It is not an archive where every design automatically deserves to remain public.

This is the Webtix-owned repository for the showcase.

## Brand

Webtix is a small Czech web-design studio aimed mainly at Czech SMEs with outdated or weak websites.

Desired positioning: premium small studio — design-led and distinctive, but approachable rather than a huge expensive agency.

The main Webtix site is `webtix.cz`.

Relevant brand traits:

- Strong editorial typography.
- Warm/off-white, black, pink and terracotta/red.
- Handwritten red annotations.
- Deliberate asymmetry.
- Direct Czech copy.
- Personality without generic agency language.
- Motion only when it has a purpose.

Avoid generic SaaS cards, meaningless huge typography, gradient-heavy AI aesthetics, repetitive layouts and Awwwards imitation.

## Portfolio philosophy

The showcase should prove:

**different business → different problem → different website**

Showing fewer excellent projects is better than showing many mediocre ones.

Existing projects may later be classified as:

- A — keep.
- B — keep but improve.
- C — archive from public showcase.

C projects should not automatically be deleted from the repository.

## Truthfulness

Never describe a concept as completed client work.

Café AdAstra is currently a real business that has received a Webtix redesign proposal and is in discussion with Webtix. Unless this status is explicitly updated later, do not call it a completed Webtix client/project.

Fictional showcase businesses must be labelled honestly as concepts.

When presenting work publicly, use one of these statuses where relevant:

- Real client work.
- Proposal for a real business.
- Webtix concept for a fictional business.
- Experimental / exploratory concept.

Do not imply a commercial relationship where none exists.
